<p align="center"><b>Benchmarking the many ways to build an AI tutor.</b></p>

TutorWays is an open benchmarking framework for AI in education. A good tutor doesn't have to be a frontier LLM: small language models, decision models that choose what a student sees next, and systems that combine them can each be the right choice, depending on the course, the budget, and the hardware. We evaluate these approaches side by side so instructors and developers can choose with evidence.

### What we evaluate

- **Models.** Proprietary and open-weight LLMs, small language models, and decision models, compared on response quality, cost, latency, and deployment tradeoffs.
- **Tutor systems.** Complete tutors, assessed on their prompts, retrieval, guardrails, and the quality of their educational responses.

### What we're building

- A reproducible test suite that runs the same tasks across every kind of model
- Recommendations that help instructors and developers pick the right approach for their educational needs
